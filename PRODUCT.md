# Energy Dashboard — Product Truth

Energy Dashboard is a small monitoring surface for exploring a synthetic building's hourly electricity load and site comparison. It is an operations visualisation demo, not a live utility meter or carbon reporting service.

## User and job

- Audience: an operator checking when a small site uses the most energy.
- Primary job: switch between day windows, inspect the load curve, and identify the largest sample site.
- Mode: operate.

## Boundaries

- All readings and sites are synthetic.
- No utility integration, IoT telemetry, billing calculation, or emissions certification is claimed.
- The first release is a read-only browser dashboard with a sample-data toggle.
